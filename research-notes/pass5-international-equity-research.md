# Highest-signal findings (read this first)

1. **No UN health body has issued a psychedelic-therapy standard of care.** WHO’s ECDD/CND work in 2024–2025 scheduled nitazenes, HHC, and carisoprodol — **not** psilocybin, MDMA, or DMT. WHO’s 2024 MNS framework and 2025–2034 traditional-medicine strategy never name PAT. INCB (2024–2025) **encourages research** while warning about unregulated wellness/tourism; it does **not** write clinical ethics.

2. **Treaties control isolated alkaloids, not most plants.** 1971 Convention Schedule I covers LSD, MDMA, psilocybin/psilocine, DMT, mescaline. Ibogaine and ketamine are **not** internationally scheduled. INCB (2010/2012, restated in later commentary) and the Official Commentary: **ayahuasca, peyote cactus, iboga shrub, and psilocybe mushrooms are not internationally controlled**; only isolated actives are. Art. 32(4) reservations for “small, clearly determined groups” in magical/religious rites are **narrow, time-of-accession, and exclude international trade**.

3. **Religious exemptions and the medical frame are legally distinct and in tension.** US: AIRFA 1994 + 21 CFR 1307.31 (peyote / NAC) vs RFRA case law (`Gonzales v. O Centro`, 2006; Oregon Santo Daime *CHLQ*, 2009). Canada: CDSA s.56 **organization-specific** ayahuasca exemptions (since 2017; Health Canada affidavit in 2025–26 Nova Scotia litigation: ~12 religious exemptions). Medicalization of the **same molecules** does not automatically protect ceremony, conservation, or Indigenous IP — and ceremony exemptions do not authorize clinics.

4. **Nagoya/CBD is the only hard international ABS tool, and it is a poor fit for synthetic medical PAT.** Gabon + Blessings of the Forest + Terragnosis/Filament (May 2023 first Nagoya-compliant iboga export) is the live pilot. **United States is not a CBD/Nagoya Party**; US patents on psilocybin polymorphs/methods (Compass) sit outside ABS. WHO TM strategy 2025–2034 and UNESCO 2005 name Indigenous rights and benefit-sharing **without** a psychedelic annex. Celidwen 8 Rs (2023) remains **COMMENTARY**, not adopted by WHO/WMA/FDA.

5. **Veterans are the political on-ramp; ethics infrastructure has not caught up.** NDAA FY2024 §723 DoD PTRCTA: two MDMA Phase 2 awards (WRNMMC/HJF; UT Health San Antonio), **enrollment not started** as of DoD’s 15 Jan 2026 report. VA: MDMA PTSD+AUD trial (Providence/Connecticut) + Compass-supported **PIVOT** COMP360 TRD±PTSD (`NCT07226232`, recruiting 2026). Texas **SB 2308** (signed 11 Jun 2025) is **research-funding law**, not treatment authorization; as of Oct 2026 HHSC says IMPACT plans **do not meet** match and IP-revenue statutory tests. **No treaty, WHO, WMA, VA/DoD CPG, or Helsinki paragraph** addresses command influence, deployability, security-clearance spillover, or **disability-rating** effects of PAT.

6. **Lykos/FDA PDAC (4 Jun 2024) is still the binding research-ethics template:** functional unblinding (~90% drug / ~75% placebo guessed assignment), expectancy, psychotherapy as co-intervention, **AE valence** (positive/euphoric effects not captured → abuse-potential gap), session **video** (FDA later asked for independent audit of recordings), dual therapist–investigator roles, independent raters who still cannot unsee unblinding. Helsinki 2024 **tightens** dependent-relationship consent, data/Taipei, post-trial access, and environmental harm — but does **not** mention psychedelics, video of non-ordinary states, or functional unblinding.

7. **Digital set-and-setting and session recording are ungoverned by international bodies.** Alberta 2023 standards (device recording + HIA access) and MAPS Code v4 (consent to record) are the only operational rules found. MacIntyre 2026 and MIT Tech Review 1 Jul 2025 document **AI trip-sitters**. No WHO, UNESCO, WMA, or FDA digital-PAT guidance.

# 1. International bodies

## 1.1 1961 / 1971 / 1988 conventions — LAW

| Instrument | What it actually does for psychedelics | URL |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Single Convention on Narcotic Drugs **1961** (as amended 1972) | Controls cannabis, coca, opium plant material. **Does not** schedule classic psychedelics. | https://www.unodc.org/documents/commissions/CND/Int_Drug_Control_Conventions/Ebook/The_International_Drug_Control_Conventions_E.pdf |
| Convention on Psychotropic Substances **1971** | Schedule I: LSD, MDMA, psilocybin, psilocine, DMT, mescaline, etc. Art. 7: manufacture/use limited to **medical and scientific** purposes under special controls. **Art. 32(4):** optional reservation, **at signature/ratification/accession only**, for wild-growing plants containing SI substances “traditionally used by certain small, clearly determined groups in magical or religious rites”; **international-trade provisions cannot be reserved**. | https://www.incb.org/incb/uploads/documents/Psychotropics/conventions/convention_1971_en.pdf |
| 1988 Trafficking Convention | Precursors (e.g. MDMA precursors). CND 68 (Mar 2025) moved PMK glycidate footnote. | CND 68 report: https://www.unodc.org/documents/commissions/CND/CND_Sessions/CND_68/68CND_Report_Advance_Version_2505206E.docx.pdf |

**Official Commentary (1971) §32-12 (as restated by ICEERS 2026 legal architecture):** inclusion of an **active principle** in SI does **not** include the plant; peyote, *Mimosa* roots, and psilocybin mushrooms were contemplated as **not** SI. COMMENTARY synthesis of LAW: https://www.iceers.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/ICEERS_Legal_Architecture_Ayahuasca.pdf

Reservations historically cited (TNI 2015 briefing, COMMENTARY on LAW): Mexico (Indigenous plant rites), Peru (ayahuasca, San Pedro), US and Canada (peyote / NAC). https://www.tni.org/files/publication-downloads/dpb_43_eng_web_19122015.pdf

**Gap:** Conventions never define “medical use” for PAT, never address psychotherapy as co-intervention, never address Indigenous benefit-sharing, and Art. 32(4) **cannot** legalize cross-border church imports (the exact fact pattern of UDV/Santo Daime).

## 1.5 CIOMS (with WHO) 2016 — GUIDANCE

*International Ethical Guidelines for Health-related Research Involving Humans* (CIOMS in collaboration with WHO, 2016). https://cioms.ch/wp-content/uploads/2017/01/WEB-CIOMS-EthicalGuidelines.pdf

Directly usable, none psychedelic-named:

- **Guideline 7 — Community engagement:** early, sustained, participatory; protocol must describe the plan; especially important for marginalized/stigmatized groups. Community agreement **does not** replace individual consent (aligned with UNESCO Art. 6(3)).
- **Social value / low-resource settings / post-trial access / vulnerability / biological materials and data** — the rest of the 2016 set. Helsinki 2024 now overlaps on post-trial (¶34) and data (¶32 / Taipei).

**Gap:** CIOMS never operationalizes FPIC for **traditional knowledge holders who are not trial participants** (Mazatec, Bwiti, Huichol, Shipibo). That is Nagoya/UNDRIP space, not CIOMS.

## 1.7 UNESCO Universal Declaration on Bioethics and Human Rights — GUIDANCE (19 Oct 2005, Paris)

https://www.unesco.org/en/legal-affairs/universal-declaration-bioethics-and-human-rights

Non-binding but the only UNESCO instrument that simultaneously names **consent, community, benefit-sharing, traditional knowledge, biodiversity, COI, and transnational research**. PAT-usable articles (UNESCO numbering in the official text; headings below match the Declaration):

| Heading | Bite for PAT |
| --- | --- |
| Human dignity; interests of the individual over science/society | Suggestibility / “inner healer” ideology |
| Consent: community agreement **cannot substitute** individual consent | Church, tribe, or Bwiti house cannot enroll a member into a trial |
| Vulnerability; privacy | Session video; military records |
| Equality, non-discrimination, cultural diversity (diversity **cannot** waive human rights) | Religious exemption vs medical safety floors (cardiac ibogaine) |
| Social responsibility and health (access to essential medicines) | Pricing $17k–$22k/dose commentary vs public systems |
| **Sharing of benefits** (developing countries; capacity-building; not improper inducement) | Nagoya-adjacent but **not** ABS law |
| **Protection of the environment, biosphere and biodiversity** — “appropriate access and utilization of biological and genetic resources” and “**respect for traditional knowledge**” | Peyote, iboga, ayahuasca, *Psilocybe* |
| Decision-making: **declare all COI** | Industry-funded investigator-therapists |
| Transnational practices: ethical review in **host and funder** states; equal participation in benefit terms; combat illicit traffic in **genetic resources** | US-funded Mexico ibogaine; Gabon export |

**UNESCO has issued no psychedelic follow-up.** IBC/COMEST files were not found addressing PAT, AI trip-sitters, or session AV.

## 2.1 Celidwen eight Rs — COMMENTARY (Indigenous-led; not adopted by WHO/WMA/FDA)

Yuria Celidwen, Nicole Redvers, et al., “Ethical principles of traditional Indigenous medicine to guide western psychedelic research and practice,” *Lancet Regional Health – Americas* (2023). https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lanam/article/PIIS2667-193X(22)00227-7/fulltext and PMC9950658.

Four clusters / eight Rs:

| Cluster | Rs | Operational demand |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Acknowledgement | **Reverence, Respect** | Medicines as sacred, not “tools”; stop reducing Spirit medicines to “psychedelics.” |
| Knowledge | **Responsibility, Relevance** | Research that serves Indigenous health priorities, not only Western TRD/PTSD pipelines. |
| IP | **Regulation, Reparation** | FPIC; no enclosure/patents on traditional medicines; benefit-sharing; compensation post facto where taken without FPIC. |
| Belonging | **Restoration, Reconciliation** | Ecosystem restoration (peyote gardens, iboga forest, ayahuasca vines, *Incilius alvarius*); stop extractive tourism. |

# 3. Religious-use exemptions vs medical frame

| Axis | Religious / traditional | Medical / research |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Legal hook | RFRA, AIRFA, 21 CFR 1307.31, CDSA s.56, 1971 Art. 32(4), Brazil ayahuasca administrative recognition | CSA Schedule I + FDA IND/NDA; CDSA + SAP; TGA AP; 1971 Art. 7 “medical and scientific” |
| Who is the rightholder | Church, tribe, “Indian,” designated congregants | Sponsor, investigator, licensed clinician, patient |
| Safety model | Sincerity + diversion controls + sometimes screening (Health Canada churches) | GCP, DSMB, REMS, two-therapist, ECG (ibogaine) |
| Conservation | Central (peyote gardens, iboga forest) | Usually ignored unless botanical drug (Filament) |
| Benefit-sharing | Nagoya/UNDRIP/Celidwen | Orphan-drug/patent exclusivity; Texas 20% state IP cut |
| Outcome that counts | Sacrament, lineage, land | MADRS/CAPS-5, durability, abuse potential |
| Cross-border | Hardest (Art. 32(4) **excludes** trade; UDV/Santo Daime litigated this) | INCB licit manufacture stats (psilocybin kg) |

**Ethics gap:** Medical PAT marketing often **borrows** Indigenous set-and-setting (altars, “sacred,” playlists, two-sitter as shamanic echo) while the legal pathway **erases** the source community. Conversely, religious exemptions are **not** a quality-and-safety system for paying wellness clients, which is exactly UNODC WDR 2024’s worry.

No international body has a **decision tree** for: (a) Indigenous patient wants PAT in a VA clinic; (b) church member also enrolls in an RCT; (c) synthetic psilocybin vs *Psilocybe* conservation; (d) whether a “spiritual but not religious” integration coach is practicing medicine.

# 6. Research ethics: blinding, dual roles, video, AE valence (Lykos/FDA PDAC)

## 6.1 PDAC / FDA — FDA ACTION (4 Jun 2024)

Primary documents:

- FDA briefing document, NDA 215455: https://www.fda.gov/media/178984/download
- Committee minutes/questions: https://www.fda.gov/media/180463/download
- Votes: **efficacy 2–9; benefits vs risks with proposed REMS 1–10.**

**Functional unblinding / expectancy (FDA’s own words):** midomafetamine produces profound alterations → “studies are nearly impossible to blind.” Post-study survey: ~**90%** assigned to drug and ~**75%** assigned to placebo guessed correctly. Monitors could deduce assignment from in-session behavior. Expectation bias “has impacted treatment effects … to some extent”; duration of that bias into long-term follow-up unknown. MAPP1 unblinding before MPLONG further contaminates durability.

**Design:** SPA “No Agreement” (9 Mar 2017) but FDA accepted **blinded centralized independent raters** via live video, blinded to visit, assignment, and AEs. Therapists **not** to do CAPS-5. ICER 27 Jun 2024 final report: still treats functional unblinding as decisive; cannot quantify under-reporting of harms. https://icer.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/PTSD_Final-Report_For-Publication_06272024.pdf

**Therapist dual role:** PDAC members worried therapist unblinding + suggestibility; wanted 2×2 drug × therapy designs; **independent** (not sponsor) training; two **licensed** therapists; onsite medical staff; external safety reporting. Sponsor briefing: therapist-investigators trained on the manual; adherence raters used **session recordings** and a 20-item checklist (“gently encouraged collaborative exploration”). https://psychedelicalpha.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Lykos-Sponsor-Briefing-Document.pdf

That is the dual-role problem in one line: the person delivering a highly suggestive intervention is a **study investigator**, paid/trained by the sponsor, while the “independent” rater is blinded to AEs the therapist may have discouraged the participant from reporting.

**AE valence / abuse potential (Safety Issue #3 in FDA brief):** studies “did not capture effects deemed **positive, favorable, or neutral**” (euphoria, elated mood) that inform HAP. “Midomafetamine effects perceived as positive were not recorded.” PDAC discussion question explicitly asked about this gap plus missing labs/QTc. Suicidality: AESI definitions, C-SSRS, SAEs in placebo and drug arms; ICER and journalists (Vox) note participant reports of **worsening suicidality** that did not appear as such in publications.

**Video:** adherence + rater reliability vs participant access vs FDA demand to **audit tapes for missing AEs**. Using the same intimate recording for (i) fidelity, (ii) safety audit, (iii) possibly sponsor defense, and (iv) future AI training is an unresolved Helsinki ¶32 problem.

**Helsinki collision:** Aday / Muthukumaraswamy-type “fixes” (psychedelic-naive only; active concealers; extra deception) can **violate** Helsinki ¶26 (adequate information) and ¶33 (placebo harm) — already flagged in Pass 4 journals note. **No REC guidance document from WMA or CIOMS on functional unblinding.**

## 6.2 What PDAC did **not** decide (still open for Compass/Usona/DoD/VA)

- Whether modest MADRS deltas (COMP360) survive the same unblinding critique.
- Whether “psychological support” (Compass) vs “psychotherapy” (Lykos) is a real methods difference or a regulatory rebrand.
- How to code **desired** mystical experience: adverse event, mechanism, or both.
- Who owns and who can subpoena Veteran session video.

# 7. What NO body currently addresses

An international or federal **instrument** (treaty, WHO guideline, WMA paragraph, FDA guidance, VA/DoD CPG, UNESCO recommendation) was **not found** for the following. Academic papers exist for some; that is **not** institutional coverage.

1. **A WHO/ECDD critical review or treatment guideline for MDMA, psilocybin, or ibogaine as medicines** (ketamine remains unscheduled internationally; surveillance only).
2. **A Celidwen- or UNDRIP-operational protocol** that FDA, EMA, TGA, or Health Canada must run before accepting a botanical or method-of-use PAT application.
3. **Nagoya/PIC/MAT for synthetic analogs and for intangible ceremonial knowledge** (playlists, touch manuals, “inner healer”).
4. **Peyote conservation as a *medical-regulator* duty** (DEA registers distributors; FDA/EMA/WHO do not model harvest pressure from any future mescaline NDA).
5. **Cross-border religious sacrament** under 1971 Art. 32(4) (structurally excluded) — churches rely on domestic RFRA/s.56 patches, not treaty reform.
6. **Religious vs medical dual-use of the same molecule in one person** (NAC member in a VA psilocybin trial; Santo Daime member on COMP360).
7. **Command influence, deployability, PRP/flight status, and security-clearance aftermath** of PAT (JAG slides ≠ ethics policy).
8. **Disability-rating / compensation interaction** with PAT outcomes (improvement *or* iatrogenic worsening).
9. **Sponsor-donated Schedule I product + sponsor training inside a national health system** (VA–Compass PIVOT).
10. **AE valence:** when a “positive” psychedelic effect is simultaneously the abuse-potential signal, the putative mechanism, and a reportable AE.
11. **Functional unblinding** as a named Helsinki/CIOMS design problem (placebo paragraph is the wrong tool).
12. **Therapist-investigator dual role** beyond generic COI (who receives the complaint of sexual misconduct: PI, sponsor, licensing board, or none — Cover Story/ICER problem).
13. **Session audiovisual records:** extra-protected psychotherapy notes vs research data vs FDA audit substrate vs AI training corpus; participant right to **refuse recording without losing trial access**.
14. **AI trip-sitters / integration chatbots** (set-and-setting as a regulated medical device vs wellness app).
15. **Post-trial and post-approval access** when the intervention is a 6–8 hour two-therapist session, not a pill (Helsinki ¶34 is underspecified).
16. **Ibogaine cardiac lethality** as an international scheduling *or* WHO alert issue (INCB notes it, does not schedule it, does not issue a safety guideline).
17. **Equity floors** (essential-medicine listing, public-payer cost-effectiveness **with** two-therapist opportunity cost, racial/Indigenous enrollment beyond “diversity statements”).
18. **Data from Mexico/Caribbean ibogaine clinics** used to justify US Veteran policy without host-country REC + funder-country REC (Helsinki ¶23 / UNESCO transnational article — rarely enforced).
