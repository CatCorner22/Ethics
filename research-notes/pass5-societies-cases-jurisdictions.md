# Pass 5 — Societies, harm-reduction codes, Oregon/Colorado, Australia AP in practice, litigation

Live open-web research notes, **11 November 2026**. Not a code, not legal advice, not a rewrite of `psychedelic-complete-research-findings.md`. Binding status is as stated by the issuer.

**How to read tags:** **LAW** statute/rule in force · **GUIDANCE** college/society/agency practice standard · **VOLUNTARY** association code with no licensing teeth unless a board adopts it · **FDA ACTION** CRL/PDAC · **COMMENTARY** journalism/preprint · **BOARD** published licensing-board disposition · **EMPTY** searched, nothing found.

## Highest-signal findings (one page)

1. **There is still no national PAT ethics code** in the US, Canada, Australia, or New Zealand. The closest *psychedelic-specific* texts are **voluntary**: MAPS Code v4 (7 Jan 2021 / PDF 22 Jun 2022), ASKP3 Ethical Code (membership), Ketamine Psychotherapy Associates KAP Code (2024), ICEERS/ADF 18 criteria (Nov 2024), Zendo peer-support ethics, Oregon/Colorado **facilitator** rules (state licenses, not medicine). Psychiatry APA **2022** position and AMA **H-100.943** (reaffirmed **A-26**) remain “investigational only / no ballot-box medicine.” Psychology APA has **no Board position** on PAT as of Oct 2026 (toolkit + division petition ≠ policy). CPA, CMA, ACP, ACNP: **no dedicated PAT position**.

2. **Sexual-boundary clocks collide.** MAPS: **never** with participant, former participant, spouse/partner, or immediate family. Oregon facilitators: **one year** after last service. Colorado facilitators: **two years**. ICEERS/ADF: **six months** after the practitioner–participant relationship ends (and “consent cannot be assumed”). APA Psychology Ethics Code: generally **two years** plus burden of demonstrating no exploitation. ASKP3 Ethical Code: **no numbered clock**; defers to state boards. A dual-licensed Oregon facilitator bound by MAPS *and* OAR 333-333-5120 is bound by the **strictest** (MAPS never) only if they remain MAPS-certified; the **enforceable** state floor is one year.

3. **Oregon is the only jurisdiction with a public, numbered disciplinary + EMS series.** 2025: 51 complaints, 60 investigations, **22 final orders** (18 civil penalties, **4 revocations**, 0 suspensions). EMS contacted **13** times / 5,935 clients (0.2%). Published orders that were read are mostly **paper/presence/advertising**, not sexual-misconduct adjudications. InnerTrek (Tom Eckert, Measure 109 chief petitioner) fined **$3,000** for leaving a group unmonitored >5 minutes and secondary-dose consent/storage failures. Training-program revocations found are **HECC paperwork** (Myco Method, Advanced Professional Certifications), not ethics-of-touch cases. OPS **approves curriculum; it does not license training schools**.

4. **Colorado split-regulator model is live but thin.** DOR Natural Medicine Division licenses businesses; **DORA** licenses facilitators. First healing-center license: The Center Origin, **31 Mar 2025**. Official DORA application table dated **2 Feb 2026**: only **7** Natural Medicine Facilitator and **2** Clinical Facilitator applications *approved* (plus 3 training licenses). Journalism that “DORA issued 124 facilitator licenses” (CBS, Apr 2025) **does not match** the Feb 2026 DORA table — treat 124 as **unverified**. **No published facilitator disciplinary file** found; enforcement energy is **unlicensed practice** (Darren Lyman / Denver co-op, DORA cease-and-desist Dec 2025, federal suit). Sexual clock **two years**; Clinical Facilitators **must** follow the **stricter** of facilitation rules and their clinical Practice Act.

5. **Australia AP scheme in practice is small, psychiatrist-gated, and almost silent on harm.** TGA FOI: unique APs **1 Jul 2023–31 Jul 2026** = **63 MDMA / 60 psilocybin** (overlap allowed). Patients reported through **31 Dec 2025**: **123 MDMA / 65 psilocybin** (FOI 26-2177 earlier snapshot 87/47 as of mid-2025). TGA told a FOI requester **zero** AP-scheme adverse events reported through Dec 2025 — that is **spontaneous reporting**, not safety. **May 2026 TGA recommendations are AP-scheme conditions/guidance, not a change to the 2023 Poisons Standard.** RANZCP called widening the therapy dyad an **“unjustifiable risk to patient safety.”** AAPi is angry the other way (clinical-endorsement lock-in for psychologists). Author of the main “real-world” preprint (Brinzei) is **Mind Medicine Australia**–affiliated — grade down.

6. **Yensen/Buisson is the only PAT trial with a complete public accountability stack — and it is incomplete.** Civil suit **settled** (Jun 2019, undisclosed). MAPS paid **C$15,000** compassionate therapy; Buisson **waived suit against MAPS**. Yensen **unlicensed** → **no psychology board**. RCMP recommended sexual-assault charges; **Crown declined** (substantial-likelihood standard). **CPSBC (BOARD, 1 Aug 2023 / published 3 Nov 2023):** Donna Dryer **irrevocably resigned**, will not reapply, reprimand: conflict/boundary violations **and continuing therapy while knowing her husband was having sex with the patient**. *Psychopharmacology* **retracted three Phase 2 papers** (Aug 2024) for undisclosed unethical conduct at that site. FDA **PDAC 4 Jun 2024** (no on efficacy; no on risk–benefit) and **CRL 9 Aug 2024** (additional Phase 3). Resilient/Lykos **NDA resubmission 2026** is company/journalism, not FDA acceptance.

7. **Ketamine boards, not Oregon/Colorado psilocybin boards, are where sexual-misconduct cases actually exist.** Virginia: **Patrick Anthony Oliver, MD**, summary suspension Feb 2024 (sexual relationship with ketamine-infusion patient; claimed treatment had ended; booster infusion occurred). Colorado: **Mark Young, MD** (Apex Ketamine), criminal sexual-contact charges + civil suit; medical board **suspended**. Ontario: **Tatiana Zdyb**, psychologist, **registration revoked** (admitted sexual relationship with ketamine client; incompetent psychedelic practice; prior undertaking to stop KAP). New Mexico Medical Psilocybin: **rules building 2026; no board cases**.

# 1. Harm-reduction / underground ethics

## 1.1 MAPS Code of Ethics for Psychedelic Psychotherapy — current text

**VOLUNTARY (MAPS protocol / certification).** Current public text is **Version 4, 7 January 2021**, hosted as PDF dated **22 June 2022**: [maps.org PDF](https://maps.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/MAPS_Psychedelic_Assisted_Psychotherapy_Code_of_Ethics_V4_22_June_2022_Final.pdf). Same v4 also at the 2021 path. **No later numbered version found on maps.org as of 11 Nov 2026.** MAPS still points to it from [maps.org/safety](https://maps.org/safety/).

**Scope (preamble):** binds “providers delivering psychedelic psychotherapy **within a MAPS protocol**.” Consequence named in MAPS commentary: loss of MAPS certification. **Does not bind** Oregon/Colorado facilitators, underground guides, or ketamine clinics unless they contractually adopt it. MAPS PBC Ethical Review Board charter (approved 26 Jan 2022) oversees the Code; it **does not replace** IRBs or licensing boards ([ERB](https://maps.org/ethical-review-board/)).

**Twelve sections (current headings):** Safety; Confidentiality and Privacy; Transparency; Therapeutic Alliance and Trust; Use of Touch; Sexual Boundaries; Diversity; Special Considerations for Non-Ordinary States of Consciousness; Finances; Competence; Relationship to Colleagues and the Profession; Relationship to Self.

**Highest-signal operative clauses (paraphrase of v4 text):**

| Topic | MAPS v4 rule |
| --- | --- |
| Touch | Consent at intake **and in the moment**; optional except imminent harm; stop-word/gesture; no sexual touch; provider must examine own motivation |
| Sex | **Do not** engage in sexual intercourse, sexual touch, or sexual intimacy with a participant, **former participant**, their spouse/partner, or immediate family, **at any point during treatment or following termination** (**lifetime / never**) |
| Dual relationships | Avoid those likely to impair judgment or exploit; if unavoidable, extra attention + supervision |
| NOSC | Greater suggestibility, manipulation, exploitation risk; no coercive practices |
| Competence | Maintain **licensure(s)** in good standing — this is the clause Yensen already failed |
| Colleague duty | If a colleague causes/likely causes substantial harm, report to licensing board **and** MAPS Ethics Officer (844) 627-7723 |
| Inner healing intelligence | Treatment philosophy, not a safety control |

**Conflict with later MAPS practice:** the Code requires licensure in good standing, yet MAPS trial design historically required **only one** of two co-therapists to be licensed (Quartz; CBC). The Code existed in earlier form from **April 2019** (MAPS Bulletin); the Buisson relationship was 2015–follow-up, **before** v4. MAPS’s 24 May 2019 public statement still treats the sex as a Code §6 violation.

**Oregon public comments:** MAPS **did** write in the Measure 109 era (Ismail Ali / Perez / Reyzin, MAPS Bulletin “Complexities with Measure 109,” 2022 — microdosing, entheogenic practitioner track, equity). OPAB’s facilitator code of conduct **says it was inspired by** ACA, APA Psychology, **and MAPS**. A standalone MAPS comment letter that *became* OAR 333-333 was **not located**; influence is **acknowledged inspiration**, not incorporation.

## 1.2 Fireside Project

**VOLUNTARY peer support, not therapy.** Psychedelic Support Line: call/text **62-FIRESIDE**, 11 a.m.–11 p.m. PT. Claim: **>40,000** conversations since 2021 ([support line](https://firesideproject.org/support-line)).

Ethics as practiced (FAQ + training page, not a numbered code):

- **Non-clinical** emotional support only; volunteers do **not** disclose outside profession on the line.
- **No** substance or provider recommendations.
- **No** contact with callers outside the line.
- **Not** 911 / **not** 988; suicidal-intent calls transferred out.
- Volunteer training **40+ hours**: ethics, confidentiality, boundaries, emergencies/suicidality, trauma-informed and culturally attuned care, self-care. Lived psychedelic experience **required**.
- **Recording/AI tension:** calls recorded after de-identification to train volunteers and **Lucy** (AI simulator); opt-out offered, including after the fact because callers may be mid-trip. Recordings “never leave Fireside” except research agreements or valid subpoena.

Trip-sitting blog (“Holding Space”): confidentiality; avoid sitting for a romantic partner or unequal power dynamic; know limits; emergency plan. **Not** a professional code.

## 1.3 Zendo Project

**VOLUNTARY peer support (festival/event).** Training Manual 2022 v1.96 (widely mirrored; MAPS copyright line still on the ethics page): [example PDF](https://mindheal.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Training-Manual-2022-v1.96-For-Web.pdf). SIT course summary: [zendoproject.org PDF](https://zendoproject.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Zendo-SIT-Course-Summary.pdf).

Peer-support ethics (manual): keep confidence unless you must escalate; ask for help; do not sit while exhausted; **ask permission before any touch** (hand on shoulder / help sitting); no special techniques without permission; **sexual advances from the guest deflected during *and after***; **sexual engagement sitter–guest unacceptable**; sitters must not make sexual advances.

**Conflict with MAPS clinical code:** Zendo allows *some* supportive touch with in-the-moment permission and **no written pre-contract**; Oregon/Colorado require **written** supportive-touch consent **before** dosing and confine touch to hugs / hands / feet / shoulders.

## 1.4 DanceSafe

**No PAT / sitter ethics code.** Organizational values: honesty about uncertainty, consent culture, nonjudgmental, not abstinence-based ([About](https://dancesafe.org/about-us/)). Core practice is **drug checking** (“red flags, not green lights”; reagents cannot prove purity/potency) ([drug checking](https://dancesafe.org/drug-checking/)). Mitchell Gomez has consulted MAPS on harm reduction. **EMPTY** as a psychotherapy ethics source. Useful as the **adulterant / nightlife** layer that medical PAT codes omit.

## 1.5 ICEERS / Ayahuasca Defense Fund

**VOLUNTARY + legal-defense screen.** “18 criteria for ethical and responsible practice,” document version **Nov 2024**: [ADF Support Criteria PDF](https://www.iceers.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/ADF-Support-Criteria-.pdf). ICEERS uses them to decide **whether to fund legal defense** — a rare **teeth** mechanism in the underground.

**Sexual rule (sharper than Oregon, looser than MAPS):** never sexually harass/abuse; **no sexual relationships, consensual or coerced**, with a current **or recent** participant, nor spouse/partner/offspring/close relative/significant friend. “Consent cannot be assumed or assured, even when the participant approaches the practitioner.” If love/consensual sex: wait **at least six months** after the practitioner–participant relationship ends; practitioner’s duty to inform participants of this ethic.

Also: do-no-harm, welfare supreme, screening (meds + mental/physical health), truthful consent including alternatives, competence limits, aftercare. ICEERS legal-support page names **sexual abuse and guru behavior** as real patterns they see ([Legal Support](https://www.iceers.org/en/how-we-help/legal-support/)).

Chacruna *Ayahuasca Community Guide for the Awareness of Sexual Abuse* is the parallel community document (cited in Kruger et al. 2025); not ICEERS.

# 2. Professional society statements (disambiguate APA)

## 2.1 American Psychiatric Association (psychiatry)

**Position still in force:** *Position Statement on the Use of Psychedelic and Empathogenic Agents for Mental Health Conditions*, Assembly May 2022 / Board July 2022. PDF: [psychiatry.org](https://www.psychiatry.org/getContentAsset/d5c13619-ca1f-491f-a7a8-b7141c800904/50c2bc4c-a8ff-46ba-95c2-0e74752d147f/Position-Use-of-Psychedelic-Empathogenic-Agents.pdf?language=en).

**Operative sentence:** “There is currently **inadequate scientific evidence for endorsing** the use of psychedelics to treat any psychiatric disorder **except** within the context of **approved investigational studies**.” Clinical treatments “should be determined by scientific evidence in accordance with applicable regulatory standards **and not by ballot initiatives or popular opinion**.” Ketamine/esketamine **expressly not the focus**.

**2024–2026 letters are comments, not a new position:**

- **23 May 2024** PDAC comment on midomafetamine: research yes; approval must meet ordinary evidentiary bar; REMS-like controls if approved ([PDF](https://www.psychiatry.org/getContentAsset/32dec1f9-1b9f-4ded-8372-365a1e4a7ab3/50c2bc4c-a8ff-46ba-95c2-0e74752d147f/APA-Letter-FDA-PDAC-MDMA-05232024.pdf)).
- **13 Aug 2026** HRSA RFI: future FDA-approved PAT must be **physician-led**, psychiatrist-informed diagnosis/selection, medical oversight, psychotherapeutic support, outcome/AE systems ([PDF](https://www.psychiatry.org/getContentAsset/620d2f9e-466b-4732-9216-56b261bf33f1/50c2bc4c-a8ff-46ba-95c2-0e74752d147f/APA-Letter-HRSA-RFI-Safe-Administration-Psychedelic-Therapies-08132026.pdf)).
- **2 Oct 2026** FDA Part 15 docket FDA-2026-N-7542: system-design letter. Training **product-specific**, not a commercial certificate; **practice of medicine** for diagnosis/eligibility; psychotherapy by **already-licensed** psychotherapy professions; **peers do not diagnose**; **more than one person** for observation (boundary-violation rationale); physician on site and immediately available for psychiatric indications, **not necessarily in the room**; remote tech **cannot** replace direct observation for coercion/boundaries; *AJP* Jan 2025 special issue cited on drug vs support vs expectancy ([PDF](https://www.psychiatry.org/getContentAsset/05427692-5e28-4d94-8ac5-ca24d48ae5aa/50c2bc4c-a8ff-46ba-95c2-0e74752d147f/APA-Letter-FDA-Psychedelic-Drugs-10022026.pdf)).

**No 2025 “APA endorses PAT” statement.** The 2022 position is the policy; 2026 letters assume **if** FDA approves, **then** physician-led medicalization — which **collides** with Oregon/Colorado adult-use facilitator models.

# 3. Oregon Psilocybin Services

## 3.1 Frame (do not medicalize)

**LAW.** Measure 109 / ORS ch. **475A**. OHA **Oregon Psilocybin Services**, not OLCC. Age 21+. **No diagnosis required.** Facilitator license ≠ medical license. Rules: **OAR ch. 333, div. 333**. Advertising may not call it therapy in ways that deceive (see Erin Johnson order). Default **nondirective** facilitation (OAR 333-333-5120(3)).

Facilitator conduct (OAR 333-333-5120, LII): duty of care of a reasonable facilitator; **supportive touch** only with **prior written consent**, limited to hugs or hands on hands/feet/shoulders; **no sex during services**; **no romantic/sexual contact with client, partner, or immediate family for one year** after last service. Training curriculum must teach vulnerability in altered states, touch consent, sexual/emotional boundaries, historical abuse of power (OAR 333-333-3060). OPAB recommendations explicitly cite MAPS/ACA/APA as inspiration ([Approved OPAB Recommendations](https://www.oregon.gov/oha/PH/PREVENTIONWELLNESS/Documents/Approved-OPAB-Recommendations.pdf)).

## 3.2 HB 2387 dual licensure — LAW

Enrolled bill: [OLIS HB 2387](https://olis.oregonlegislature.gov/liz/2025r1/Downloads/MeasureDocument/HB2387/Enrolled). Governor signed **22 May 2025** (ch. 147). Dual-licensure operative **1 Jan 2026**.

Seven boards: LPC/LMFT, naturopathic, psychology, medical, nursing, social work, **pharmacy**. A facilitator also licensed by one of those boards may provide **health or behavioral-health services during preparation and integration** after notifying OPS (TLC) and the client. **Administration session remains facilitated adult use**, not a clinical hour under the other license. Boards **may not discipline** members **for lawfully providing psilocybin services as a licensed facilitator**. Information-sharing: health boards may disclose otherwise confidential information to OHA for 475A enforcement.

**Ethics collision:** HB 2387 **imports clinical interventions into prep/integration** while administration stays non-therapy. Colorado already had Clinical Facilitator + “follow the stricter code.” Oregon copied the dual-license *privilege* without copying Colorado’s **two-year** sexual clock.

## 3.4 EMS / adverse events — how to read them

13 EMS / 5,935 clients is **acute, on-premises, reported**. It does **not** capture delayed psychological harm, integration abandonment, sexual boundary violations after discharge, or out-of-state clients who crash elsewhere. Clients may opt out of 303 data. Low EMS ≠ FDA pharmacovigilance. All-time figures cited in the complete-findings file (32 EMS through 29 Jun 2026) were **not re-audited** in this pass.

## 3.5 Training-program issues

- **Structural:** curriculum approval ≠ school license; HECC overlay caused a **class of revocations** in 2024–25 for paperwork, not pedagogy.
- **InnerTrek** is both a major training vendor **and** a service center; the $3,000 order is **service-center facilitation**, not a curriculum revocation.
- Training may award **accelerated credit** to lineage/legacy practitioners (OHA facilitator fact sheet) — an equity tool that also **weakens a uniform competence floor**.
- 2026 listening sessions: live-music limits, fee hikes, dual-licensure implementation — not sexual-ethics complaints ([2026 summary](https://www.oregon.gov/oha/PH/PREVENTIONWELLNESS/Documents/2026-Public-Listening-Sessions-Summary.pdf)).

# 5. Australia authorised prescriber scheme **in practice**

## 5.1 What is law vs May 2026 recommendations

**LAW (unchanged since 1 Jul 2023):** MDMA Schedule 8 for **PTSD** and psilocybin Schedule 8 for **TRD**, **psychiatrist Authorised Prescribers** only, SAS/AP scheme + HREC. Not a marketing authorisation; not Oregon-style adult use.

**May 2026 TGA “final recommendations”** after targeted consultation (83 invited / 29 responses): [TGA Citizen Space](https://consultations.tga.gov.au/medicines-regulation-division/targeted-consultation-ap-scheme-for-psychedelics/). TGA also posted “Updates to AP scheme requirements” (cited by Brinzei). These are **TGA operational conditions on APs**, **not** a rescheduling Act and **not** RANZCP bylaws.

Four clusters:

1. **Psychiatrist competency:** trial experience **and/or** supervision by an experienced AP; RANZCP to define supervisor criteria (TGA admits few psychiatrists have trial experience).
2. **Therapy dyad:** at least **one** of: Psychology Board **clinical endorsement**, Medical Board general registration, NMBA **with mental-health experience**, Occupational Therapy Board — **plus** PAT in scope, skills as determined by the **AP psychiatrist** under HREC. AP decides additional practitioners.
3. **AP physically present on site at administration** (not the entire 8–10 h day); AP owns screening and **ongoing** consent; remains responsible if they leave.
4. **Site:** medically supervised, rescue meds, S8 storage, **within 15 minutes of an ED**.

**Not yet / contested:** PACFA/counsellors as lead; general-registered psychologists without clinical endorsement (AAPi “deeply disappointed”). RANZCP: widening the dyad = unjustifiable safety risk. **EMPTY:** parliamentary confirmation that all four clusters are already hard-coded in every active AP letter — treat as **TGA policy direction that APs are expected to meet**, not as a 2023-style scheduling instrument.
