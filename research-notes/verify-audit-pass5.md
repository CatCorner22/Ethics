## Most important corrections

1. **Canada 5-MeO-DMT is not CDSA Schedule III.** DMT is Sch. III item 7; 5-MeO-DMT is unnamed. Health Canada treats it as a new psychoactive substance whose **sale** is under the Food and Drugs Act.
2. **Canada ketamine is not “Sch. I, Part 1 item.”** It is CDSA **Schedule I, item 14(1)** (ketamine under phencyclidine).
3. **Alper 2012 fatality review is *Journal of Forensic Sciences*, not *Am J Addict*.** Jalal 2013 is the *Am J Addict* case report.
4. **Breeksema 2020 is *CNS Drugs* (systematic review), not *Front Psychiatry*.** The TRD-psilocybin qualitative paper is *Scientific Reports* 2024; *Front Psychiatry* 2022 is oral esketamine.
5. **Nagele 2021 is *Science Translational Medicine*, not *Biological Psychiatry*.** 2015 *Biol Psychiatry* is correct.
6. **TMB May 2026 §§173.6–173.18 were withdrawn 29 June 2026.** Live text is a **July 2026 reproposal of §§173.6–173.17**, still **not adopted**.
7. **Smith & Sisti:** online first 27 May 2020; **print *JME* 2021;47(12):807–814.** Process file’s “2020” is the online year, not the print citation.
8. **Stage labels are stale:** LSD is **Phase 3** (Voyage/Emerge/Panorama), not “2b/3”; GH001 is **post–Phase 2b** (hold lifted 5 Jan 2026); SPL026 Phase 2a is **completed** and published.

### 24. Oregon / Colorado consent rule specifics — **CONFIRMED**

### 25. CAP / CPSA recording-of-active-phase wording — **CONFIRMED**
