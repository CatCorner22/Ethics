### 1. Hype, expectancy, blinding

**Olga Khazan, “The Weak Science Behind Psychedelics,” *The Atlantic*, 18 Oct 2024.**  
https://www.theatlantic.com/ideas/archive/2024/10/psychedelics-medicine-science/680286/  
Khazan (who says she previously contributed to positive coverage) argues that the renaissance was built on small samples, true-believer investigators, illegal-status research barriers, and underreported AEs, while Pollan’s book/Netflix series sold a “safe solution.” Live fetch of the Atlantic page returned **403**; summary is from search snippets and secondary reprints (CALM USA, DB Recovery). **NEW** as a named long-form. **Opinion/analysis** grounded in the 2024 Lykos year; **UNCERTAIN** on every in-article anecdote not independently opened.

**Jonathan Lambert, “Psychedelics Are Challenging the Scientific Gold Standard,” *The Atlantic*, 3 Jun 2024.**  
https://www.theatlantic.com/health/archive/2024/06/psychedelics-mdma-ptsd-fda-placebo/678588/  
On the eve of PDAC: functional unblinding is either a fatal flaw in PAT evidence or a crack in RCTs for mind-acting drugs. Quotes field researchers on both sides. **PARTIAL** (unblinding is already in PDAC notes). **Well-supported description of the methodological dispute**; whether RCTs are “the problem” is **opinion**. Page **403** on live fetch.

**Balázs Szigeti et al., “The difference between ‘placebo group’ and ‘placebo control’,” *Scientific Reports*, 2023.**  
https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-023-34938-7  
Computational argument for **activated expectancy bias (AEB)**: a placebo *group* is not a placebo *control* if patients can tell they dosed. Re-analysis of a self-blinding microdose trial suggests observed microdose–placebo gaps are at risk of being false positives; microdosing as **active placebo**. **NEW** as media/science criticism (not in the YouTube notes). **Well-supported methods paper**; generalizing from microdosing to macrodose PAT is **interpretive**.

**Szigeti / Carhart-Harris group, expectancy in COMP360 vs escitalopram, *Psychological Medicine*.**  
https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/psychological-medicine/article/assessing-expectancy-and-suggestibility-in-a-trial-of-escitalopram-v-psilocybin-for-depression/6EB6D496BB09903D45BDDECBF9030351  
Patients expected more from psilocybin than from SSRI; expectancy predicted SSRI response, **not** psilocybin response; trait suggestibility predicted psilocybin response. Authors say PAT may be *less* expectancy-vulnerable than critics assume. **NEW** as a **counter-criticism from inside trials**. **Well-supported n=55 exploratory**; does not erase PDAC unblinding numbers. Flag as **field disagreement**, not a refutation of ICER.

**Balázs Szigeti & colleagues, “Expectancy Effects in Psychedelic Trials,” *Biological Psychiatry: CNNI*, 2024.**  
https://www.biologicalpsychiatrycnni.org/article/S2451-9022(24)00055-7/abstract  
Review: as dose and public enthusiasm rise, so does bias risk; expectancy is also a physiological process that can be measured and (in care) harnessed. **NEW** review. **Well-supported as a literature review**; policy conclusions are **opinion**.

**Kai Kupferschmidt, *Science*, 5 Jun 2024 / 11 Jun 2024 / 12 Aug 2024.**  
https://www.science.org/content/article/fda-advisory-panel-rejects-mdma-ptsd-treatment  
https://www.science.org/content/article/reeling-fda-setback-researchers-plot-future-psychedelic-assisted-medicine  
https://www.science.org/content/article/fda-rejected-mdma-assisted-ptsd-therapy-other-psychedelics-firms-intend-avoid-fate

### 2. Adverse events, measurement, publication ethics

**ICER, *Midomafetamine-Assisted Psychotherapy for PTSD* Evidence Report, 14 May 2024.**  
https://icer.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/PTSD_Revised-Report_For-Publication_05142024.pdf  
https://icer.org/news-insights/press-releases/institute-for-clinical-and-economic-review-publishes-evidence-report-on-treatment-for-post-traumatic-stress-disorder/  
Publicly available MAPP1/2 data would look promising (CAPS-5 MD −10.2 vs Lykos psychotherapy+placebo) **if valid**; ICER rates evidence **“I” (insufficient)** because of functional unblinding, prior beliefs of investigators, and inability to judge biased reporting of benefits/harms. No health-benefit price benchmark. David Rind: regulators with primary data must decide. **ALREADY** as a one-line “insufficient”; **NEW** as the primary PDF citation with the “LSNAP” control-arm language and the “therapists encouraged favorable reports” allegation. **Well-supported HTA**. Lykos disputes bias.

**Olivia Goldhill, STAT, 31 May 2024 and 9 Jun 2024.**  
https://www.statnews.com/2024/05/31/mdma-therapy-ptsd-lykos-fda-advisory-meeting-psychedelics/  
“The inside story of how Lykos’ MDMA research went awry” (STAT, 9 Jun 2024; paywalled; indexed on Goldhill’s site).  
Former employees describe lapses; FDA reviewer David Millis called missing **euphoria/positive-effect** capture for abuse-potential “a major concern.” Earlier Goldhill work: participant severe SI after MDMA **not in published AE tables** (Lykos: events “captured appropriately” in FDA datasets). **PARTIAL** (Goldhill is named in YouTube notes without article-level inventory). **Well-supported investigative**; unpublished-dataset claims remain **UNCERTAIN** without the NDA. Exact 9 Jun slug **UNCERTAIN** (paywall; confirmed via RamaOnHealthcare reprint and Goldhill index).

**Quartz / Goldhill, “Psychedelic therapy has a sexual abuse problem,” ~Mar 2020.**  
https://qz.com/1809184/psychedelic-therapy-has-a-sexual-abuse-problem-3/  
Pre-Cover Story paper trail. **ALREADY** referenced in YouTube notes. **Well-supported journalism**.

**McNamee, Devenot, Buisson, “Studying Harms Is Key…,” *JAMA Psychiatry*, 29 Mar 2023 (print May 2023).**  
https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamapsychiatry/article-abstract/2802941  
AEs poorly defined; distinctive PAT risk is **therapist–patient interaction under NOSC**; COI-free harm research. **ALREADY** in journals/Pass 5. Included here because it is the **named clinical-criticism** that later media cites. **Well-supported viewpoint** (not a systematic trial).

**Breeksema et al., mixed-methods AE review, *J Psychopharmacol* / PMC9548934, 2022.**  
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9548934/  
44 papers, 598 patients: AEs often not systematically assessed; nausea/headache/anxiety common; one SAE (PVCs); selection excludes high-risk patients. **PARTIAL**. **Well-supported systematic review**.

**Palitsky et al., AE assessment framework, *J Psychopharmacol*, 2024.**  
https://doi.org/10.1177/02698811241265756  
54 potential PAT-specific AE constructs; existing scales miss post-acute worldview/spiritual harms. **NEW**. **Well-supported methods/consensus**.

**Andrew Jacobs, “Three MDMA Studies Are Retracted…,” *NYT*, 12 Aug 2024.**  
https://www.nytimes.com/2024/08/12/health/mdma-ptsd-retractions.html  
*Psychopharmacology* retracts three papers after authors confirmed they knew of Canadian-site unethical conduct and still included the data, plus incomplete MAPS/Lykos COI. Lykos: not in the NDA; wanted corrections; complained to COPE. STAT first reported the retractions. **PARTIAL** (Pass 5 literature table). **Well-supported**.

**Protocol vs publication discordance, *Journal of Clinical Epidemiology*, 2026.**  
https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0895435626000454  
Of 336 MDMA/psilocybin registrations: major POM changes 17.6%, eligibility changes 28.6%; 72% of completed trials posted no ClinicalTrials.gov results; among 29 with both posted results and papers, only **three** fully concordant on AEs. **NEW**. **Well-supported**; live access was abstract-level.

**Reuters, 23 Jun 2025; Fierce Biotech Phase 2b and Phase 3 coverage; BioSpace COMP006.**  
https://www.reuters.com/business/healthcare-pharmaceuticals/compass-pathways-depression-drug-succeeds-late-stage-trial-2025-06-23/  
https://www.fiercebiotech.com/biotech/psilocybin-clears-biggest-test-yet-improving-depression-phase-2b-but-compass-stock-falls  
https://www.fiercebiotech.com/biotech/compass-guides-psychedelic-therapy-second-pivotal-depression-win-triggering-filing-plan  
COMP005: MADRS −3.6 vs placebo at week 6 (below ~5-point Street hopes); shares plunged >46%. Phase 2b already had **suicidal behavior in 3/25 mg arm** among non-responders. Later COMP006: 3.8-point vs 1 mg; DSMB chair: no clinically meaningful suicidality imbalance; BioSpace notes a **suicide 80 days after COMP360**, investigator-unrelated. Findings doc already records modest MADRS numbers as pipeline fact. **NEW as market/media criticism of hype**. Company IR is **not** independent safety adjudication. Reuters page **401** on live fetch; numbers from search snippets + Fierce.

**FDA compounding risk alert, 10 Oct 2023 (ketamine, including oral).**  
https://www.fda.gov/drugs/human-drug-compounding/compounding-risk-alerts
